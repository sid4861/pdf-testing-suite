PDF Suite · design system · v1

Proof

A quieter system for a tool whose job is to be believed. Neutral surfaces, one accent for action and selection — and every other colour on screen means a change on the page.

Colour means change

Red, green, orange and violet are reserved for removed, added, moved and restyled. Chrome stays grey; the accent marks what you can act on or have selected.

Numbers carry units

Measurements are set in Plex Mono with tabular figures and always say in, pt or %. A finding names its page, and its source cell when it has one.

Page first

The rendered page is the largest thing on screen. Lists sit beside it and link by hover; nothing hides the page except a dialog the user opened.
